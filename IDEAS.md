# Personalized Service Platform (name TBD)

## Core Idea

A platform where users customize **what** a service does and **how** it delivers — tailored to individual taste.

## How It Works

1. A **project** provides a base service (e.g., vocabulary learning, news digest, fitness tracking).
2. Users interact with the service and discover they want it shaped to their preferences.
3. The platform lets them **configure features and delivery channels** — chat, email, push notifications, etc.

## Example: Vocabulary Learning

- User picks what to learn: words, phrases, idioms, with explanations and examples.
- User picks how to receive it: Telegram bot, email summary, web dashboard.
- The service adapts accordingly — already prototyped as `vocab-chat-service`.

## Key Principles

- **User-driven**: preferences drive the experience, not defaults.
- **Channel-agnostic**: same service, multiple delivery methods.
- **Project-agnostic**: the platform layer works across different service domains.
